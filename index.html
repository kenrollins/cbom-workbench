One weakness, from finding to fixed

The last change that landed toward post-quantum, with every verdict re-deriving from its raw output: records-db made to require a hybrid post-quantum key exchange. Each step says who acts, what happened, and what the CBOM gives it; every step is an event on a signed ledger (12 ML-DSA-65 checkpoints on the change run alone). A model acts in 2 of the 13, each in a role it earned by measurement, and never where the question is whether the change worked.

  3. 3Ranked rule

    The OMB M-26-15 rules mark the service prioritized (high-impact, high-value-asset, sensitive-in-2030; the data must stay secret through 2101) and open a key-establishment item in queue kex-2030, due 2030-12-31: DH-2048, RSA, X25519 are quantum-vulnerable; X25519MLKEM768 is offered but not enforced.   4. 4Traced to its cause model AI · trace-to-cause

    The CBOM's part The model is handed one CBOM finding and the files the CBOM links to its service, not the estate. What it says is recorded as a claim beside the evidence, never as a fact.
